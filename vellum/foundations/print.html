Physical Document Architecture Invariants
  • 0.5pt Hairline Grids: Document tables, letterhead rules, and telemetry callouts strictly use 0.5pt (0.175mm) solid black borders for laser and offset printing.
  • Zero Digital Artifacts: Background blurs (`backdrop-filter`), the luminous lamps, and soft drop-shadows are strictly stripped in print stylesheets.
  • Substrate Recommendation: 350gsm premium uncoated bright white cotton cardstock for executive memos; 120gsm matte bond for technical multi-page specifications.
  • Document Metadata Rails: Every printed sheet must include standard header/footer telemetry: DOC ID, REVISION, SECURITY STATUS, and PAGE X OF Y.
