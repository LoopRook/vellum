Core surface & canvas roles

Every label below is read from tokens.json at render time, so a swatch and its caption cannot disagree.

Brand palette

The project's colours, each exactly as given, and the lamps they light. A brand brings as many colours as it has; which one lights each lamp is chosen per mode, because light to dark does not always carry over (GRAMMAR §3.3, §3.5). These are Vellum's own; a project replaces them.

Status colours

The core five, read from tokens.json. A fixed status keeps its job and may be recoloured only inside its family's hue range; a free one is named by the project and may take any hue a free status may sit at, a brand colour included. A project may also add its own (GRAMMAR §3.9).

Tonal ramps

The full palette. A project may reach for any step; unused steps are expected — shipping the whole ramp is the point.
