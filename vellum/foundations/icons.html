Icon Invariant Rules (Swiss Precision)
  • Never decorate with floating cards: Icons are not standalone cards; they live inside .btn-icon triggers or inline next to text.
  • Automatic currentColor: Icons must always use stroke="currentColor" so they effortlessly inherit the text color of their parent element.
  • Proportional Sizes: 14px for badges & pills, 16px for button triggers, 20px for primary console controls.
