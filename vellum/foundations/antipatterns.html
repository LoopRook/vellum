Brand Invariants & Anti-Patterns

Interactive visual guide codifying strict brand rules ("DO's") and prohibited mistakes ("DON'Ts").

02 / Typography & Headline Hierarchy TYPOGRAPHY
✓ DO: High-Contrast Monochrome

VELLUM
DESIGN LANGUAGE

Display headlines are strictly ink () or white ().

Rule: High-contrast black and white maintains crisp architectural authority.
✕ DON'T: Rainbow / Tinted Titles

VELLUM
DESIGN LANGUAGE

Tinting headline text yellow or cyan degrades contrast and looks unprofessional.

Mistake: Color is strictly reserved for small precision telemetry dots, not massive headline type.
03 / Pill Category vs. Status Grammar PILL SYSTEM
✓ DO: Strict Separation
TYPOGRAPHY CONTROLS LIVE

Neutral pills for structural categories; tinted pills strictly for live state.

Rule: Clear semantics prevent visual confusion between categories and operational state.
✕ DON'T: Semantic Pollution
TYPOGRAPHY CONTROLS HARDWARE

Coloring static category names like "Typography" in green or amber pollutes the status grammar.

Mistake: If everything is green or amber, status indicators lose their meaning.
04 / Form Focus States & Hardware Inputs CONTROLS & FORMS
✓ DO: Monochrome Ink Focus

Focus state transitions smoothly to crisp ink border (border-color: var(--color-text-primary)).

Rule: Precision hardware tools use clean mechanical focus transitions.
✕ DON'T: Generic SaaS Blue Rings

Generic blue halos (#3B82F6) clash with the Swiss palette.

Mistake: Never leave default browser focus outlines or soft generic SaaS rings.
