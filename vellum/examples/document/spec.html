02 / Rules on Every Page GRAMMAR §2
  • Pill Grammar: Neutral untinted pills (.tag-pill) are strictly reserved for structural categories (e.g. TYPOGRAPHY, CONTROLS). Tinted pills (red, amber, emerald, cyan, purple) are reserved for live state.
  • Quiet Hover: Interactive cards rise by translateY(-2px); in light mode the edge brightens, in dark the rise is the whole gesture. A coloured glow on hover is never allowed.
  • Monochrome Focus Rings: Form fields and sliders take a clean ink border (border-color: var(--color-text-primary)) plus a monochrome halo (--color-focus-ring), never the browser's blue ring.
  • Responsive Stacking: Data tables collapse into a stack of cards on screens 540px wide or narrower.
