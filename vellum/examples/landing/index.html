SPECIFICATION

The System at a Glance

Typography & Scale
  • Display Headline: Geist 900, tracked at --tracking-display.
  • Section Titles: Geist 800, tracked at --tracking-title.
  • Body Type: Inter 400 for running prose.
  • Labels: JetBrains Mono 700 uppercase.
Surfaces & Sheets
  • Stage sheet: 40px backdrop blur, with a 1px edge in light mode.
  • Tray: 20px blur, denser than the sheet.
  • Card Hover: rises 2px; never a glow.
  • Lighting: soft lamps behind the page, painted by the light table.
Color & Modes
  • Light: #F4F6F9 canvas ground.
  • Dark: #18181B near-black ground.
  • Tonal Ramps: nine rungs per hue, derived at fixed L* targets.
  • Mode Choice: remembered between visits.
Controls & Interaction
  • Segmented Pills: the active one turns solid ink.
  • Pill Grammar: untinted is a category, tinted is live.
  • Range Slider: 18px handle, track filled to the value.
  • Header: hides on scroll down, returns on scroll up.
